# MinMaxCal privacy policy

MinMaxCal processes your calendars and reminders on your device. It
has no developer-operated server, analytics, advertising or tracking
and does not send your calendar or reminder data to its developer.
This policy covers the iPhone, Mac and Apple Watch apps.

## Calendar and reminder access

With your permission, MinMaxCal reads events, reminders and their
details from your system accounts to display the agenda. Full Calendar
access is required to read events. On iPhone, events are read-only.
The iPhone and Mac apps can complete reminders and undo completions.
The Apple Watch app only reads events and reminders.

On Mac, optional calendar mirroring copies the fields you select into
your chosen destination calendar. Your system accounts synchronise
calendar and reminder changes with their providers. Anyone who can
read a shared destination calendar may see the copied fields.
MinMaxCal does not operate those accounts or their servers.

## Local storage and exports

Preferences, calendar selections and matching rules stay in the app's
local storage. The Mac also stores the identifiers and times needed
for dismissed or snoozed takeovers and calendar mirroring ownership.
These records do not store event titles, notes or attendees. Takeover
records older than a day are removed when the ledger is saved; ended
mirroring records are pruned during syncing. Preferences remain until
changed or the app's local data is removed.

Event and reminder content is held in memory while the app runs.
The Mac's **Export Report** action saves calendar content, identifiers,
settings and diagnostics to the location you choose. Reports are not
sent automatically. You control whether to share them and when to
delete them. Device backups and any syncing of exported files follow
your system and storage provider settings.

Optional Mac meeting coaching captures microphone and system audio only
after an explicit recording choice. Audio is processed locally and is
never saved or uploaded. Saved transcripts, summaries, statistics and
speaker names expire 48 hours after recording ends by default. Change the
retention period to 48 hours, 7 days or 30 days in Coaching settings, or
delete individual meetings.
Expired meetings are removed while coaching runs or when next enabled.
Copied transcripts and backups must be removed separately.

## Links and other apps

Opening a meeting, location, notes link or support link passes it to
the chosen app or website. That service handles the request under its
own privacy policy. The Mac checks installed link handlers locally so
you can choose which app opens meeting links.

## Your choices

Revoke Calendar or Reminders access in system privacy settings at any
time. You can change the selected calendars and disable mirroring in
MinMaxCal's Settings. Turning mirroring off leaves existing copies in
place. Remove copies before clearing the Mac's local data if you want
MinMaxCal to retain the ownership information needed to remove them.
Calendar entries and reminders remain in your system accounts unless
you change or delete them there.

Deleting the iPhone app removes its local data; offloading it retains
that data. On Mac, removing the app alone leaves preferences and
ledgers in its sandbox container. To remove them, quit the app and
remove its MinMaxCal container from your user Library's Containers
folder. Exported reports and backups must be removed separately.
